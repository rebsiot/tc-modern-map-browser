# Third-Party Content, Access Requirements, and Rights

**This is an unofficial, independently developed Foundry VTT utility for managing Scene compendiums that the user has acquired and installed separately.** It is not a map pack, content subscription, downloader, or a substitute for creator-provided files.

## Bring your own legitimately acquired content

- This repository and its downloadable module ZIP contain **software code and mapping metadata only** (creator pack identifiers, source Scene IDs, location/category names, floor and variant labels). They do **not** include battlemap images, artwork, map assets, thumbnails, tokens, creator Scene/Journal documents, original Foundry compendium databases, creator ZIPs, or Patreon account credentials.
- To browse or import a particular creator's maps, you must independently obtain and install the corresponding **Foundry-ready Scene module/compendium** in your own Foundry installation, and enable it in the relevant world. A bulk download of image files alone will not supply walls, lights, Regions, or native teleporter configuration.
- Some packs and Foundry-ready modules require a qualifying **Patreon membership/tier**, a purchase, or another permission from the original publisher. Other packs may be available under separate free-content licenses. **A subscription is not automatically required for every pack**, nor does it by itself confer permission to redistribute content. Check the specific map's acquisition route and terms.
- This module **does not authenticate to Patreon or a creator's site**, bypass access restrictions, collect subscriber files, or download third-party packs. Users retain their own content and manage access directly with the publisher.
- Missing creator packs simply have no corresponding playable source Scenes to import; the reviewed built-in catalog cannot create missing scenes or artwork.

- Paid or patron-exclusive battlemaps, image variants, textures, thumbnails, map assets, tokens, creator-produced Journals or Scene data, packaged compendiums, or original creator module ZIPs.
